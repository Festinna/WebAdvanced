🍕 Pepperoni Pizza Recipe

🕒 Preparation Time
Prep time: 20 minutes
Cook time: 15 minutes
Total time: 35 minutes

🧾 Ingredients
1 pizza dough
½ cup pizza sauce
1½ cups mozzarella cheese (shredded)
20–30 pepperoni slices
1 tablespoon olive oil
½ teaspoon dried oregano
½ teaspoon garlic powder
👨‍🍳 Instructions
Preheat the oven Set your oven to 220°C (425°F).
Prepare the dough Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface into a round shape.
Add the sauce Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust.
Add cheese Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
Add pepperoni Place the pepperoni slices evenly on top of the cheese.
Season Sprinkle oregano and garlic powder, and drizzle a little olive oil.
Bake the pizza Put the pizza in the oven and bake for 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.

Serve Let it cool for a few minutes, slice, and enjoy! 🍕



🍝 Spaghetti Recipe


🕒 Preparation Time
Prep time: 10 minutes
Cook time: 20 minutes
Total time: 30 minutes

🧾 Ingredients
200 g spaghetti
1 tablespoon olive oil
1 small onion (chopped)
2 cloves garlic (minced)
200 g ground beef
1 cup tomato sauce
Salt (to taste)
Black pepper (to taste)
Parmesan cheese (optional)

👨‍🍳 Instructions
Cook the spaghetti Boil water in a pot, add salt, and cook the spaghetti for 8–10 minutes until tender.
Cook the meat Heat olive oil in a pan. Add chopped onion and garlic and cook for 2–3 minutes.
Add ground beef Add the ground beef and cook until it turns brown.
Add tomato sauce Pour the tomato sauce into the pan and mix well. Let it simmer for 10 minutes.
Season Add salt and black pepper to taste.
Combine Drain the spaghetti and mix it with the sauce.
Serve Put the spaghetti on a plate and sprinkle Parmesan cheese on top if you like.

🍽️ Serving Tip: Serve your spaghetti with garlic bread or a salad.



🍗 Homemade Chicken Nuggets

📝 Ingredients
2 chicken breasts
1 cup breadcrumbs
½ cup flour
2 eggs
1 teaspoon salt
½ teaspoon black pepper
½ teaspoon garlic powder
½ teaspoon paprika
Oil (for frying)

👨‍🍳 Instructions
Prepare the chicken Cut the chicken breasts into small nugget-sized pieces.
Prepare the coating
In one bowl add flour.
In another bowl beat the eggs.
In a third bowl mix breadcrumbs, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
Coat the nuggets Dip each chicken piece in flour, then egg, and finally coat it with the breadcrumb mixture.
Cook the nuggets Heat oil in a pan and fry the nuggets for 3–4 minutes on each side until they are golden and crispy.
Serve Serve hot with ketchup, mayonnaise, or your favorite sauce.

🍽️ Tip
For a healthier version, bake the nuggets in the oven at 200°C (400°F) for about 18–20 minutes instead of frying.



🍣 Sushi Recipe

🕒 Preparation Time
Prep time: 30 minutes
Cook time: 15 minutes
Total time: 45 minutes
🧾 Ingredients
2 cups sushi rice
3 cups water
4 tablespoons rice vinegar
1 tablespoon sugar
½ teaspoon salt
4 sheets nori (seaweed)
1 cucumber (cut into thin strips)
1 avocado (sliced)
200 g fresh salmon or tuna
Soy sauce (for serving)
👨‍🍳 Instructions

Cook the rice Wash the sushi rice and cook it with water for about 15 minutes.
Season the rice Mix rice vinegar, sugar, and salt into the cooked rice. Let it cool.
Prepare the nori Place a sheet of nori on a bamboo sushi mat.
Add the rice Spread a thin layer of rice over the nori.
Add fillings Place cucumber, avocado, and fish in a line across the rice.
Roll the sushi Carefully roll the sushi using the bamboo mat and press gently.
Cut the roll Use a sharp knife to cut the roll into 6–8 pieces.
Serve Serve with soy sauce for dipping.



🥗 Caesar Salad Recipe

📝 Ingredients
1 head romaine lettuce
½ cup croutons
¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
1 chicken breast (optional)
2 tablespoons olive oil
Salt and black pepper
🥣 Caesar Dressing

½ cup mayonnaise
1 tablespoon lemon juice
1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
1 garlic clove (minced)
2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
Salt and pepper to taste
👨‍🍳 Instructions

Prepare the lettuce Wash and chop the romaine lettuce, then place it in a large bowl.
Cook the chicken (optional) Season the chicken breast with salt and pepper. Cook it in a pan with olive oil for about 6–7 minutes per side, then slice it.
Make the dressing In a small bowl mix mayonnaise, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, garlic, Parmesan cheese, salt, and pepper until smooth.
Assemble the salad Add the lettuce, croutons, and sliced chicken to the bowl. Pour the dressing on top and toss well.
Finish and serve Sprinkle extra Parmesan cheese on top and serve immediately.
🍽️ Tip:
For extra flavor, add a squeeze of fresh lemon and a little more Parmesan cheese before serving.



Greek yogurt pancakes recipe 🥞

Ingredients
1 cup Greek yogurt
1 large egg
½ cup flour
1 tsp baking powder
1 tbsp sugar or honey (optional)
½ tsp vanilla extract (optional)
Pinch of salt
1–2 tbsp milk (if batter is too thick)
Butter or oil for cooking
Instructions

Mix the wet ingredients In a bowl, whisk together the Greek yogurt, egg, vanilla, and sugar/honey.
Add dry ingredients Stir in the flour, baking powder, and salt until combined. Add milk if the batter feels too thick.
Heat the pan Place a non-stick pan on medium heat and add a little butter or oil.
Cook the pancakes Pour small amounts of batter (about 2–3 tablespoons) into the pan. Cook for about 2 minutes until bubbles appear, then flip and cook another 1–2 minutes.
Serve Enjoy with toppings like:
Honey
Maple syrup
Fresh fruit
Nuts
✅ Tip: Greek yogurt makes pancakes fluffier and higher in protein than regular pancakes.
