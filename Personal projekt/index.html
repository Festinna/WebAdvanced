🍕 Pepperoni Pizza Recipe


Ingredients: Pizza dough, tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese, pepperoni.

Steps: Preheat oven to 220°C.
Spread sauce on dough,
add cheese and pepperoni,
then bake for 12–15 minutes.

🍝 Spaghetti Recipe

Ingredients: Spaghetti, tomato sauce, olive oil, garlic, salt.

Steps: Boil spaghetti until soft.
Heat oil, cook garlic, add sauce.
Mix with spaghetti and serve.

🍗 Homemade Chicken Nuggets

Ingredients: Chicken breast, eggs, breadcrumbs, salt, oil.

Steps: Cut chicken into pieces,
dip in egg,
coat with breadcrumbs,
then fry until golden.

🍣 Sushi Recipe

Ingredients: Sushi rice, nori, fish or vegetables, soy sauce.

Steps: Cook rice,
place on nori, add filling, roll tightly,
then slice and serve.

🥗 Caesar Salad Recipe

Ingredients: Romaine lettuce, croutons, parmesan cheese, Caesar dressing.

Steps: Chop lettuce,
add croutons and cheese,
pour dressing on top,
then toss and serve.

Greek yogurt pancakes recipe 🥞

Ingredients: Greek yogurt, eggs, flour, baking powder, honey.

Steps: Mix all ingredients into a batter.
Pour onto a hot pan, cook until golden on both sides,
then serve.
